Help

  • K, numerator & denominator fields take plain maths — e.g. K 1, 1 over 1+10^{-4}s, or K 10*(1+s/10)… — or LaTeX like \frac{1}{1+s} in either field
  • Click on a plot with a Zero/Pole tool to place; click again on the same spot to increase its order (×2 → +40 dB/dec…)
  • Shift + hover a marker (or just hover while placing) shows its raw power as a dotted line on the plot you're hovering — hidden when the pointer leaves the plot
  • Del removes the selected element · Esc switches back to the Select tool
  • Mouse wheel over a plot zooms the ω axis; over an axis (the margins with the labels) it scales just that axis
