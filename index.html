Help

  • Accepts e.g. \frac{1}{1+10^{-4}s}, 10*(1+s/10)/(s*(1+s/1000)), 2*10^4/(s^2+1.4*s+100)
  • Click on a plot with a Zero/Pole tool to place; click again on the same spot to increase its order (×2 → +40 dB/dec…)
  • Shift + hover a marker (or just hover while placing) shows its raw power as a dotted line on the plot you're hovering — hidden when the pointer leaves the plot
  • Drag the line vertically → gain · drag a marker → corner frequency · background drag pans · wheel zooms ω
  • Ctrl (while the solution is shown) switches it to the real Bode plot
  • Keys: V select · Z zero · P pole · C/X complex · D delete · F fit · Del remove selected · Esc back to select
